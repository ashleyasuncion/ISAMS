Platform Overview

What is ISAMS?

ISAMS replaces fragmented manual workflows and unstructured cloud storage with a unified, intelligent platform that covers every major administrative and academic process at the College of Computer Studies.

Archiving

Thesis & HTE Archiving

Centralized digital repository with search, research analytics dashboards, and NLP-based similarity detection to ensure originality across all submissions.

Submissions

Faculty Requirement Submission

Replaces unstructured Google Drive with a rule-based portal featuring auto-validation, smart syllabus auditing, and one-click clearance certificate generation.

Discipline

Student Violation Management

Logs offenses with evidence attachments and automatically suggests penalties based on violation severity and frequency, with full analytics dashboards.

Lab

Laboratory Time Monitoring

Barcode kiosk entry with ML-enhanced identity verification, real-time station mapping, predictive maintenance scheduling, and one-click attendance exports.

Analytics

Data Analytics Throughout

Every module ships with dashboards, performance comparisons, traffic forecasting via linear regression, and exportable PDF/Excel reports for academic governance.

Green School Design System

"Institutional Integrity by Design" — ensuring every module produces a unified, consistent interface.

Built with React v19 + Vite + Tauri v2, styled with Tailwind CSS v4, powered by Supabase.
